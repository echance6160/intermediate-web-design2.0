As my typography project I wanted to make a movie review of Spider-Man Brand New Day I personally really enjoyed this movie.

The way peter parker had adjusted to his new life was quite impactful and also quite sad but it showed what he goes through to keep the people he loves safe and the lengths he's willing to take to see that through.

Im excited to see what all they do next in terms of character and I liked seeing MJ and Ned having very incorporated roles in this movie and how he slowly builds his relationship back with them.

The movie deals with the topic of isolation and inspires viewers to go out and make new connections and really spending time with friend's and family which I find to be quite the message.
